# Cloud session: application pack for one role

Replace ROLE_ID with an id from `hunt/roles.json` (e.g. `gera-deal-analyst`).

---
Build `packs/ROLE_ID/` for the role ROLE_ID in hunt/roles.json. Open its `src` URL and read the full JD first.
1. `jd_map.md`: map every line of the JD to evidence the candidate actually has, or mark it as a gap. Don't stretch.
2. `resume_bullets.md`: 6–8 bullets rewritten in the JD's own vocabulary. Include only claims that can be defended. No GRIHA; the PDC is in progress.
3. `cover_note.md`: under 150 words and specific to this firm's recent deals or projects (cite the sources).
4. `interview.md`: 20 likely questions with answer outlines, including 5 technical ones (FSI/TDR, IRR, JDA vs outright, title checks, approvals).
5. `outreach.md`: a LinkedIn note to the likely hiring manager's team. Draft only; do not send.
Commit on a branch and open a PR.
