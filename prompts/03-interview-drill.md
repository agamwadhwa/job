# Cloud session: interview drill bank

---
Create `drills/` with one file per developer function (land & title, approvals, JDA, feasibility, construction finance, asset management, capital raising). Each file needs:
- 10 concept questions, then 5 numeric problems with full worked answers. Use Pune/Mumbai numbers: ready reckoner rates, UDCPR FSI, premium FSI, TDR, RERA timelines.
- 3 case prompts ("a landowner offers 40% area share on 2 acres in Wagholi. Evaluate it.").
- A "common traps" section.
Build `drills/quiz.py`, which picks 10 random questions and reveals answers on Enter. Commit and open a PR.
