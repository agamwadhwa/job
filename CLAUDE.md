# Job hunt workspace

This repo supports a job search for real-estate developer-side roles in Pune and Mumbai. The target start date is May 2027 (MBA graduation).

## Target functions (the 12 developer functions)
land & title · approvals & liaison · JDA structuring · feasibility & underwriting · design coordination · project & construction finance · procurement & contracts · delivery · cost control & RA billing · institutional leasing · asset management & monetization · capital raising

Priority order for roles: land/BD, feasibility & underwriting, JDA/redevelopment, liaison & approvals, then RE capital markets, fund investing, construction finance and asset management.

## Files
- `hunt/scoring.py`: weights and formulas. **Do not change the weights without being asked.**
- `hunt/roles.json`: role snapshot (public postings only).
- `reports/ranking.md`: generated by `python -m hunt.rank`. Never edit it by hand.
- `prompts/`: ready-made instructions for cloud sessions.
- `model/`: feasibility model work (see prompts/01).

## Rules for any session in this repo
1. Honesty: never fabricate experience, certificates, hours, or contacts. Only put claims on a resume that the candidate can defend in an interview. GRIHA is **not** held. The Permaculture Design Certificate is **in progress**.
2. Verify before adding: a role goes into `roles.json` only after its posting has been opened. Put the URL in `src`. Record the experience floor honestly.
3. Screening: keep roles whose verbs are sourcing, underwriting, structuring, feasibility, approvals, valuation, investment or lending. Drop sales, presales, channel-partner "sourcing" and site-execution roles.
4. Never send messages, applications or emails for the candidate. Draft only.
5. Keep personal details, family business details and contact names out of this repo while it is public.
6. After changing `roles.json`, run `python -m hunt.rank` and `python -m pytest -q`, then commit both.
7. Style: direct and dense, with no filler and no moralising. Mark any speculation as speculation.

## Live tracker
The main tracker is a claude.ai artifact (Hunt Ledger). Scheduled scans update it on Mon, Wed and Thu. This repo is its versioned mirror and the place for heavier builds (models, application packs).
