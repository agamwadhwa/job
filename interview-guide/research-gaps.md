# Research gaps: what couldn't be verified, and what you can paste in

This guide was built in a cloud container where **AmbitionBox and Glassdoor pages were blocked by the network proxy**. Logging in wouldn't have helped: the block is at the network level. Research used search-result summaries and news/company sources instead. That's why most "likely questions" in the playbooks are labelled "typical for this role type (unverified for this firm)".

## Highest-value pages to open yourself
Open these while logged in and copy the **interview** sections (role, date, rounds, questions asked) into a text file. Then in a Claude session say: "update the playbook for <firm> with these interview reports, citing them". Only questions from real reports should be attributed to a firm.

## Facts marked "verify" that matter most in interviews
- UDCPR TDR table by road width, premium % of ASR, ancillary % of ASR (for the specific authority and zone).
- DCPR 2034: 33(7)(B) text, fungible premium rate, Table 12 FSI by road width.
- MahaRERA 70% account withdrawal mechanics (Forms 1/2/3) and lender repayment from the designated account.
- RBI rules on bank lending for land acquisition.
- Stamp duty and metro cess in PMC/PCMC and Mumbai. JDA stamp duty and GST on development rights.
- Section 79A redevelopment directives: current consent thresholds.
- Hinjawadi Phase 2 planning authority (MIDC vs PMRDA), Wagholi's PMC merger status, ring-road and metro Line 3 status.
- Tata Capital listing/merger status. Godrej group structure after the 2024 settlement.

Gera (land deals), Avener, Chaphalkar Karandikar, Karandikar Life Spaces, Tatvm, Akshay Group, DTG, Aspect (acquisition date), Oberoi (Pune plans: weak source), C&W (named mandates). Check MahaRERA registrations and company LinkedIn pages before interviews.
