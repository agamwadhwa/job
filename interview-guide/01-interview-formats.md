# How each firm type interviews

**What the evidence covers.** Public interview reports for Indian real-estate roles are thin and mostly cover sales, site and design roles, not land or underwriting roles. AmbitionBox and Glassdoor pages could not be opened from the build container. Aggregate figures below come from search-result summaries of those pages, and each has a link. Everything else is marked **typical (unverified)**. It reflects how these roles are commonly run in India and should be checked against people in the teams. See `research-gaps.md` for what to verify.

## 1. Listed developers

**Rounds (typical, unverified per firm):**
1. **HR screen** (20–30 min). Checks CV match, notice/availability, CTC, location, and motivation for the developer side.
2. **Functional round with the BD/land manager** (45–60 min). Covers FSI, TDR, premium, carpet vs built-up, JDA structures, land title basics, and one quick feasibility ("2 acres in X, what would you pay?").
3. **Senior round with the BD head/CFO/IC member** (30–60 min). Tests judgement: which deal to walk away from, how to handle a landowner who won't budge, and what drives IRR.
4. **Final with a promoter/director or HR head** (15–30 min). Fit, stability, compensation.

**Evidence:**
- Lodha: public reports describe about 3 rounds: HR with basic questions, JD matching, and personality/psychometric tests, then Manager and VP rounds. These are mostly for non-BD roles. ([Glassdoor, Lodha interviews](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Lodha-Interview-Questions-E299920.htm)) Indeed respondents rated the difficulty "medium", and about a third heard back within a week. ([Indeed, Lodha Group](https://in.indeed.com/cmp/Lodha-Group/interviews))
- Sunteck: one report describes resume shortlisting, then a group discussion, then a personal interview. ([Glassdoor, Sunteck](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Interview/Sunteck-Realty-Interview-Questions-E686172.htm)) A Sunteck BD JD lists what the role is tested on: parcels under JV/outright/DM, access and DP roads, frontage, nearby pricing and sales velocity, projections with justified assumptions, and coordinating due diligence. ([Sunteck BD JD mirror](https://bebee.com/in/jobs/business-development-residential-land-acquisition-sunteck-realty-ltd-mumbai--tideri-5fb4bfbe-0755-437c-bc12-4e85488577d2))
- Oberoi: summary of Glassdoor data: 50% positive experience, difficulty about 2.2/5, no role breakdown. ([Glassdoor, Oberoi](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Oberoi-Realty-Reviews-E519755.htm))

**What each round tests:**
- The functional round tests whether you can translate a plot into sellable area and money without an Excel sheet. Drill: REF-W area statement in under 5 minutes, verbally.
- The senior round tests whether you know what kills deals: title, access, reservations, society consent, approvals timeline and absorption.
- The final round tests whether you'll stay. Two years of stability matters more to them than brilliance.

## 2. Mid-size promoter-led developers

**Rounds (typical, unverified):** HR or a consultant screens you (sometimes by phone), then a department head, then the promoter/MD. For roles posted via placement consultants (DTG via Azzulo, the SRA liaison role via Amber HR), the consultant screen is round one.

**Evidence:** at Gera, one Glassdoor report (a design/architecture role, not BD) lists an HR "tell me about yourself" round, an hour-long technical round and a situational round. The hardest question reported was "How long are you planning to stick around with us?" ([Glassdoor, Gera](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Interview/Gera-Developments-Interview-Questions-E938771.htm))

**What's different:**
- The promoter round is informal and decisive. Expect "Where do you stay? Do you know Wagholi? Who's your family? What will you do in your first 90 days?"
- These firms test local knowledge more than theory: micro-market rates, which roads are coming, which landowners are known.
- Excel tests are rare. You will be asked to work numbers out on a notepad in front of them.
- Six-day weeks and alternate Saturdays are common. Gera's roles.json entry records "WFO, 6-day alt Sat".

## 3. IPCs (ANAROCK, JLL, CBRE, Cushman & Wakefield, Colliers, Knight Frank)

**Rounds (typical, unverified):**
1. HR/TA screen.
2. Team round (associate director/manager): technical questions for the vertical. Capital markets: IRR, cap rate, yield on cost, and how a land sale is marketed. Valuation: the three approaches and RR rates. Consulting: market sizing, absorption, a supply pipeline.
3. Test: a timed Excel test (60–120 min) is common for capital markets and valuation analysts. Examples: build a DCF for a leased office, compute an IRR, run a sensitivity. Some firms send a take-home market note instead.
4. Head of vertical/city MD: fit, client handling, deal awareness.

**Evidence:**
- ANAROCK: Glassdoor aggregate about 72.5% positive, difficulty 2.69/5, average process about 6 days, based on around 40 reports across all roles. Sales roles dominate. ([Glassdoor, ANAROCK](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Interview/Anarock-Interview-Questions-E1905306.htm))
- JLL ranked #1 for India investment advisory in MSCI RCA 2025 (US$4.3 bn advised, about 48% share). Expect questions on the capital-markets pipeline. ([JLL newsroom](https://www.jll.com/en-in/newsroom/jll-ranks-1-in-india-for-real-estate-investment-advisory))

**What's different:** IPCs value speed, clean Excel and writing. A short market note on a micro-market is the best preparation artefact, and `prompts/05-deal-note.md` builds one.

## 4. RE funds and NBFCs

**Rounds (typical, unverified for Indian funds):**
1. HR/recruiter screen.
2. Associate/VP technical: IRR vs multiple, waterfall basics, structured debt vs equity, security cover, escrow, RERA 70%, DSCR, and how you'd diligence a developer.
3. **Modelling test or case.** Global RE-PE practice ranges from a 1-hour on-site test to a take-home case over several days. The typical exercise is to model an acquisition from price, leverage, rent and an exit assumption. ([Wall Street Prep, RE interview guide](https://www.wallstreetprep.com/knowledge/real-estate-interview-guide/); [A Student of the Real Estate Game](https://astudentoftherealestategame.com/what-to-expect-and-how-to-prepare-for-an-interview-in-real-estate-private-equity-part-ii/)) Indian residential funds typically use a construction-finance or structured-equity case instead of an office acquisition (unverified per firm).
4. Presentation to a partner/IC member.
5. Partner fit round. Sometimes a second partner.

**NBFC credit and technical roles (Tata Capital technical valuation, a private bank RE credit role):** a technical round on the valuation approach, stage-of-construction certification, cost-to-complete, and approved-plan vs as-built. Then a credit head round. Excel tests are less common than at funds (typical, unverified).

**Context to know before a Kotak Alts conversation:** the 14th RE fund had its final close at about US$1 bn in June 2026. It is anchored by an ADIA subsidiary (about US$675 mn+), with NPS Korea's first India alternatives commitment. The stated focus is growth and development financing across residential and commercial. ([Realty n More](https://realtynmore.com/kotak-alts-secures-1-billion-adia-and-nps-korea/); [Enterprise AM](https://enterpriseam.com/mena-india/2026/06/05/adia-writes-a-usd-675-mn-check-for-kotaks-real-estate-fund/))

## 5. Valuation firms

**Rounds (typical, unverified):** technical round → practical exercise → practice head. Questions: the three approaches (sales comparison, income, cost/DRC), ready-reckoner rates and their use, and valuing land with and without approvals. Also: the difference between valuation for bank lending, for IBC, and for financial reporting (Ind AS 113). Sthapatya describes itself as a team of IBBI registered valuers and chartered engineers doing land, property and plant valuation, feasibility and project reports. Expect questions on how a site inspection report is written. ([Sthapatya](https://www.sthapatyaindia.com/))

**Your edge:** 14 months on site. You can read a drawing and judge stage of completion, which most MBA freshers can't.

## 6. Redevelopment firms

**Rounds (typical, unverified):** redevelopment head → promoter. Tested on:
- The society offer: additional carpet to members (%), corpus per member, rent per month for the transit period, and shifting charges.
- Consent: the 51% general body resolution for appointing a developer under the Section 79A directives. **Verify current.**
- 33(7)(B) incentive FSI in Mumbai. The Pune equivalent is UDCPR provisions for redeveloping old buildings. **Verify current.**
- How you'd handle a member who refuses to vacate.

Evidence of firm focus: Chaphalkar Karandikar markets redevelopment in Model Colony ([CK Developers](https://ckdevelopers.in/redevelopment/)). Karandikar Life Spaces lists revised carpet and rent reimbursement as member benefits ([Karandikar](https://karandikar.co.in/redevelopment/)). DTG's Dahisar project is a society redevelopment ([DTG Avenue 68](https://dtgdahisar.com/)). Rustomjee's FY26 business development was all redevelopment ([TipRanks](https://www.tipranks.com/news/company-announcements/keystone-realtors-posts-record-pre-sales-doubles-redevelopment-pipeline-in-fy26)).

## 7. Campus drives (NICMAR)

NICMAR's placement season runs from July–August to May–June, with mock tests, simulated GDs and mock interviews. ([NICMAR Pune placements](https://www.nicmar.ac.in/pune/department/placements)) Aggregated NICMAR interview reports describe a telephonic round on experience, a technical round with scenario questions, and a senior-management round. ([Glassdoor, NICMAR](https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/NICMAR-Interview-Questions-E511578.htm)) Campus recruiters often run a GD or aptitude test first, then 1–2 interviews on the same day (typical, unverified).

**GD topics to prepare (typical):**
- Will redevelopment solve Mumbai's housing shortage?
- Plotted development vs apartments on the Pune fringe.
- Should RERA escrow drop from 70% to 50%?
- REITs for residential in India.
- Is TDR making Pune costlier?

## Durations and timelines (typical, unverified)

- Developer: 1–3 weeks from first call to offer. Promoter availability sets the pace.
- IPC: 1–3 weeks. The ANAROCK aggregate is about 6 days (see above).
- Fund: 3–8 weeks with the modelling test.
- Campus: same day to one week.
