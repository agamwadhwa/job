# Case 03: a society redevelopment in Andheri under 33(7)(B)

## Prompt
A 38-year-old cooperative housing society in Andheri West: 48 flats, average 550 sq ft carpet (26,400 sq ft existing carpet), existing BUA about 2,700 sq m, on a 3,000 sq m plot on an 18 m+ road. The society's PMC has invited offers. A competitor has offered **45% additional carpet + ₹8 lakh corpus per member**. What's your offer?

**Assumptions (all verify current):**
- Permissible FSI under DCPR 2034 (zonal + premium + TDR) = 2.5.
- Fungible compensatory area 35%, premium at 50% of ASR.
- Additional FSI premium at 50% of ASR. ASR land ₹1,40,000/sq m.
- TDR ₹5,000/sq ft.
- Sale ₹34,000/sq ft carpet. Construction ₹4,500/sq ft on construction area = 1.5× BUA.
- Carpet = 90% of BUA including fungible.
- Rent ₹45,000/month × 42 months. Shifting ₹1 lakh/member.
- Statutory ₹300/sq ft of BUA. Professional fees 5%. Marketing + admin 7%. Finance ₹8 cr.

## 5-minute framework answer
1. **Buildable:** 3,000 × 2.5 = 7,500 sq m + 35% fungible = 10,125 sq m ≈ 1.09 lakh sq ft BUA → **98,086 sq ft carpet**.
2. **33(7)(B) incentive:** max(15% × 2,700, 10 × 48) = **480 sq m**, free of premium. It reduces TDR needed.
3. **Members at 40%:** 36,960 sq ft rehab → **61,126 sq ft to sell** → ₹208 cr revenue.
4. **Costs ₹153 cr → profit ₹55 cr (26.4%)** at 40%. **Maximum offer at a 20% margin ≈ 62%.**
5. **Offer:** 45% carpet (matching) + ₹10 lakh corpus + rent with 10% annual escalation + a bank guarantee + a 36-month construction timeline with a delay penalty. Win on certainty, not on a higher %.

## Full numbers

**Area:**
- Permissible BUA 7,500 sq m. Fungible 2,625 sq m. Total 10,125 sq m = **1,08,984 sq ft**.
- Carpet (90%) = **98,086 sq ft**.

**Rehab at 40%:** 26,400 × 1.40 = 36,960 sq ft → sale carpet 61,126 sq ft → revenue **₹207.8 cr**.

**Costs (₹ cr):**

| Fungible premium (sale share only) | 11.45 | 2,625 × 62% × 50% × 1,40,000 |
| Rent | 9.07 | 48 × 45,000 × 42 |
| Corpus | 4.80 | 48 × ₹10 lakh |
| Shifting | 0.48 | |
| Statutory | 3.27 | |
| Professional fees | 3.68 | |
| Marketing + admin | 14.55 | 7% |
| Finance | 8.00 | |
| **Total** | **152.9** | |

**Profit ₹54.9 cr = 26.4%.**

**Margin by offer (corpus ₹10 lakh):** 30% → 28.9%; 40% → 26.4%; 50% → 23.7%; 60% → 20.6%; 70% → 17.3%. **Max at a 20% margin ≈ 62%.**

**Price shock:** at a 40% offer, a 10% lower sale price (₹30,600) → margin **19.0%**. That's why you don't bid to the maximum.

**Competing offer check:** at 45% + ₹8 lakh, margin ≈ 25% (interpolated), so the competitor's bid is rational, not reckless. You can match it.

**How to win at 45%:**
- **Corpus** ₹10 lakh (costs ₹0.96 cr more than ₹8 lakh).
- **Rent** at market + 10% annual escalation, paid quarterly in advance.
- **Bank guarantee:** e.g. 10–15% of rehab construction cost.
- **Timeline** 36 months from vacating, with a delay penalty (e.g. rent + ₹X/month per flat).
- **Specification** annexed to the PAAA.
- **Parking** 1 per member.

## What a strong candidate says that a weak one misses
- **Computes the incentive correctly** (the higher of 15% and 10 sq m per tenement) and recognises it's **within** permissible FSI. It saves premium and TDR, not extra area beyond permissible.
- **Checks the plot-to-existing ratio** first: existing BUA 2,700 on a plot permitting 10,125 with fungible is a healthy 3.75×. Dense societies (ratio < 2.5×) rarely work.
- **Knows the society fears non-completion** more than it wants 5% more carpet, and designs the offer around that.
- **Stresses the price** before bidding. At 60%+, a 10% price fall wipes the margin.
- **Names the approvals path:** society GB resolution under the Section 79A directives, PMC tender, developer appointment, development agreement + PAAAs, IOD (under 33(7)(B)), CC, and RERA for the sale component. Plus the height check (Andheri West may be within airport height limits: **verify with NOCAS**). Flags that height clearance can cap usable FSI.
- **Mentions conveyance:** confirms the society has conveyance or deemed conveyance. Without it, the title is weak.
- A weak candidate answers "offer 50% to beat them" without costing it, or forgets rent and corpus.
