# Case 06: a warehouse pre-lease on the Chakan corridor

## Prompt
A developer has 12 acres on the Chakan–Talegaon corridor (industrial zone) at ₹2.25 cr/acre. A 3PL tenant offers a **built-to-suit pre-lease for 2.4 lakh sq ft**: 9-year lease, 5-year lock-in, ₹26/sq ft/month, 15% escalation every 3 years. Should the developer build? What's the asset worth on completion, and what's the equity return with LRD?

**Assumptions:**
- Construction ₹1,850/sq ft. Soft costs and finance during construction 12% of construction. Stamp on land 7%.
- NOI = 95% of rent (owner-borne leakage). Market cap rate 8% (range 7.5–8.5%).
- LRD at 60% of cost, 9.5%, interest-only for simplicity.
- 5-year hold, exit at 8% on year-6 NOI.

## 5-minute framework answer
1. **Cost:** land ₹27 cr (+ stamp) + construction ₹44.4 cr + soft ₹5.3 cr = **₹78.6 cr**.
2. **NOI:** 2.4 lakh × 26 × 12 = ₹7.49 cr rent → **₹7.11 cr NOI** → **yield on cost 9.05%**.
3. **Value at 8%:** ₹88.9 cr, so **₹10.3 cr of value created** (13% on cost). Spread is 105 bps: thin.
4. **Returns:** unlevered 5-year IRR **14.0%**. With 60% LRD at 9.5%: **equity IRR ~19.6%**, DSCR 1.59× (interest-only).
5. **Recommendation:** build, but only with the 5-year lock-in, a strong 3PL covenant, and a hard cost cap. Negotiate rent to ₹27–28 or cut cost to lift YoC above 9.5%.

## Full numbers

**Cost:**
- Land 12 × 2.25 = ₹27.0 cr (+ stamp ₹1.89 cr)
- Construction 2,40,000 × 1,850 = ₹44.4 cr. Soft 12% = ₹5.33 cr.
- **Total ₹78.6 cr**

**Income:**
- Rent years 1–3: ₹7.49 cr. Years 4–6: +15% = ₹8.61 cr.
- NOI years 1–3: ₹7.11 cr. Years 4–5: ₹8.18 cr.

**Valuation:** at 8% = ₹88.9 cr. At 8.5% = ₹83.7 cr (only ₹5 cr above cost).

**Unlevered cash flow (₹ cr):** Y0 −78.6. Y1–Y3 +7.11. Y4 +8.18. Y5 +8.18 + TV (8.18 ÷ 0.08 = ₹102.2 cr) → **IRR 14.0%**.

**Levered:**
- Debt ₹47.2 cr (60%), interest ₹4.48 cr/year. Equity ₹31.4 cr.
- Equity cash flow: −31.4. Then (NOI − interest) each year. At exit, TV − ₹47.2 cr debt → **IRR 19.6%**.
- Interest cover (DSCR, interest-only) = 7.11 ÷ 4.48 = **1.59×**. An amortising LRD over 12 years would pull DSCR near 1.1–1.2×, so check the lender's minimum.

**Risk checks:**
- **Tenant exits after lock-in (year 5):** re-leasing downtime (6–9 months), brokerage, and possibly lower rent. Exit value at year 5 is really a bet on renewal. Discount the TV for vacancy risk.
- **BTS specificity:** high clear height and floor load suit most 3PLs, but a custom layout may not.
- **Supply:** Chakan–Talegaon has a large warehousing pipeline (check the IPC industrial report). Rent growth may be flat.

## What a strong candidate says that a weak one misses
- **Uses the yield-on-cost vs cap-rate spread** to judge development value, and notes that 105 bps is thin. Most developers want 150–250 bps.
- **Separates lock-in from lease term**, and treats years 6–9 income as contingent.
- **Checks land use:** industrial zone, MIDC vs private land (MIDC plots are leasehold, which needs MIDC transfer permission: **verify**), access road width for trailers, and power availability.
- **Tenant credit:** 3PL balance sheets vary. A parent guarantee or larger security deposit may be needed.
- **Exit route:** sale to an institutional warehousing platform or REIT at a stabilised yield. Asks who the likely buyers are.
- A weak candidate computes value at 8% and says "yes, profitable", without the spread, lock-in risk or the leverage check.
