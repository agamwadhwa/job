## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [land] How much land for a 50 MWp solar park, and what do you look for?
Answer pointer: about 200–250 acres at 4–5 acres/MWp (assumption). Flat, non-irrigated, low-shadow land; close to a substation with evacuation capacity; clear title; road access.

### Q2 [land] Lease vs buy for solar land?
Answer pointer: a lease (25–30 years) lowers capex and suits farmers who want to keep ownership. Buying gives control but needs NA and ceiling checks. Lenders need a registered lease with tenure beyond the loan.

### Q3 [land] Aggregating 250 acres from 60 farmers: biggest risks?
Answer pointer: holdouts, joint/undivided family holdings, Class II/tribal restrictions, and disputes discovered late.

### Q4 [land] What's in a 7/12 extract?
Answer pointer: Village Form 7 (ownership, rights, encumbrances, mutation references) and Form 12 (crop and cultivation details). Mutation entries are in Form 6.

### Q5 [appr] What approvals does a solar park need on the land side?
Answer pointer: land use permission/NA (or the exemption applicable to renewables: verify current), grid connectivity approval, and local body NOCs.

### Q6 [firm] Group captive: explain it to a manufacturer.
Answer pointer: the consumer holds ≥ 26% equity in the generating company and consumes ≥ 51% of its power, which gives exemption from certain surcharges (verify current rules).

### Q7 [land] Right-of-way for a 15 km transmission line: how?
Answer pointer: compensation for tower footprint and corridor, under the applicable state policy. Negotiation village by village.

### Q8 [behav] Why renewables land if your target is real estate?
Answer pointer: honest. Land and title work is the same skill, and scale aggregation experience is rare.

### Q9 [land] How does land cost affect a solar tariff?
Answer pointer: land is a modest share of capex (about 3–6%: assumption), but delays in land acquisition push commissioning, which can mean penalties under the PPA.

### Q10 [land] How do you engage a village community?
Answer pointer: gram panchayat meetings, clear compensation, local employment commitments, and consistent follow-through.
