## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [am] Define DSCR, and compute it: NOI $1.2 mn, annual debt service $950k.
Answer pointer: 1.2 ÷ 0.95 = 1.26×. Affordable housing lenders often want ≥ 1.15–1.20× (assumption).

### Q2 [am] What's debt yield and why do lenders like it?
Answer pointer: NOI ÷ loan amount. It's independent of interest rate and amortisation, so it's a clean measure of risk.

### Q3 [am] What's LIHTC?
Answer pointer: the US Low-Income Housing Tax Credit. Investors get tax credits for funding affordable rental housing with rent/income restrictions. Citi CCC is a major lender/investor in this space (general knowledge; verify specifics).

### Q4 [am] Walk me through a rent roll review.
Answer pointer: units, occupancy, in-place vs market rent, lease expiries, concessions, and arrears.

### Q5 [am] NOI: what's included and what's not?
Answer pointer: revenue minus operating expenses. Excludes debt service, capex (usually, though reserves may be deducted), depreciation and income tax.

### Q6 [cf] What covenants would you monitor on a multifamily construction loan?
Answer pointer: completion date, budget/cost overrun, lease-up milestones, DSCR at conversion, and reporting deadlines.

### Q7 [cap] Project finance analyst: what's a cash-flow waterfall in a solar project?
Answer pointer: revenue → O&M → senior debt service → DSRA top-up → subordinated debt → equity distributions.

### Q8 [cap] What's a DSRA?
Answer pointer: a debt service reserve account, typically 1–2 quarters of debt service.

### Q9 [behav] Why US real-estate credit from Mumbai when your background is Indian construction?
Answer pointer: honestly, underwriting discipline is transferable and valuable. Don't overstate your fit.

### Q10 [cf] A property's DSCR drops from 1.30× to 1.05×. What do you do?
Answer pointer: diagnose (occupancy, expense spike, rate reset), check covenants, notify the underwriter, and consider a cash trap or remediation plan.
