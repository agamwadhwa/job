## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [firm] All five FY26 additions were redevelopment. Why would Rustomjee lean so hard on redevelopment?
Answer pointer: no upfront land cost, so capital-light; supply of old societies in the suburbs; Rustomjee's brand trust with societies; DCPR incentives. Risk: long approvals and member disputes.

### Q2 [jda] A cluster in Lokhandwala: what's different from a single-society redevelopment?
Answer pointer: multiple societies, so multiple consents; possibly 33(9) cluster provisions if eligible (verify); larger scale gives better design and amenity; more complex negotiation.

### Q3 [appr] Liaison ET: which authorities handle which redevelopment type in Mumbai?
Answer pointer: MCGM for 33(7)(B) societies; MHADA for MHADA colonies under 33(5); SRA for slum schemes under 33(10); the Mumbai Board for cessed buildings under 33(7). **Verify current.**

### Q4 [appr] What's a premium in Mumbai approvals and when is it paid?
Answer pointer: charges for additional FSI/fungible FSI/relaxations, paid to MCGM at approval stages, often allowed in instalments (verify current).

### Q5 [land] Plotted role: what's your title check sequence for fringe agricultural land in Thane district?
Answer pointer: 7/12 and mutation history, 30-year search, NA status, tribal land (Section 36A MLRC: verify), forest/ESZ checks, and access.

### Q6 [feas] Why launch plotted development in MMR now?
Answer pointer: (speculation) demand for second homes/plots near infrastructure (Atal Setu, the new airport, the Virar–Alibaug corridor), low capex and quick sell-through.

### Q7 [jda] Om Nagar CHSFL: what is a CHSFL vs a CHSL?
Answer pointer: CHS "Ltd" vs a "Federation Ltd" (a federation of multiple societies). **Verify the expansion** before using it.

### Q8 [behav] Liaison means patience and precision. Example?
Answer pointer: STAR from contracting, e.g. getting a bill or measurement approved by a government engineer. [FILL]

### Q9 [feas] ₹143 cr for FSI: why would a developer buy FSI rather than land?
Answer pointer: to max out a site already controlled. TDR/FSI purchase lifts sellable area without new land cost. Works if the sale rate minus construction cost covers the FSI cost with margin.

### Q10 [cap] Pre-sales grew 33% but BD grew 118%. What does that tell you?
Answer pointer: pipeline is building faster than sales. Future launches depend on redevelopment approvals converting on time.
