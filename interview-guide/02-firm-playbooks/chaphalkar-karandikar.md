## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [jda] A 1985 society in Model Colony: 24 flats of 700 sq ft carpet on a 1,200 sq m plot on a 12 m road. Build the offer.
Answer pointer: see the worked example in 03-technical/03, problem 2. Existing carpet 16,800 sq ft. Offer 35–40% more (assumption for core Pune). Check that the free-sale area after UDCPR FSI + TDR + ancillary covers cost, rent and margin.

### Q2 [appr] Which UDCPR provisions help old-building redevelopment in Pune?
Answer pointer: UDCPR has specific incentives for redeveloping old/dangerous buildings, plus TDR loading by road width. Know that incentives exist and cite "UDCPR redevelopment provisions (verify current clause and incentive %)". Don't bluff the numbers.

### Q3 [jda] How is a developer chosen by a Pune society?
Answer pointer: the society appoints a PMC or architect, runs a feasibility, invites tenders, and the general body approves by majority. Cooperative department directives under Section 79A (verify current) set the process.

### Q4 [land] What's in the society's title file you'd check?
Answer pointer: the conveyance deed, property card/7/12, the original sanctioned plan, the OC, the society registration, any mortgages on individual flats, and pending disputes.

### Q5 [feas] Rent: 24 members at ₹30,000/month for 36 months. What's the total, and how do you reduce the risk?
Answer pointer: 24 × 30,000 × 36 = ₹2.59 cr (plus escalation). Reduce risk by phasing and faster approvals, and by paying rent quarterly in advance only after the vacating date.

### Q6 [jda] Two members refuse to vacate. What now?
Answer pointer: the majority GB resolution, talking one-to-one about their specific concern, and the society's own dispute route. Never threaten. Legal remedies exist under co-op law (verify current) but are slow.

### Q7 [feas] Corpus fund per member: why does it exist and how do you size it?
Answer pointer: it compensates members for higher maintenance and property tax. Sized as a fixed amount per member or per sq ft. Competes against the carpet % in the offer.

### Q8 [behav] Why redevelopment and not greenfield?
Answer pointer: core-city land doesn't come any other way. The skill is negotiation plus feasibility, which you want.

### Q9 [appr] What's the risk from the transit period for the developer?
Answer pointer: approval delays extend rent; RERA registration of the sale component; and sale can't start until approvals.

### Q10 [firm] Why would a society pick a mid-size local developer over a national brand?
Answer pointer: local trust, flexibility on members' specific asks, and faster decisions. But national brands offer balance sheet and brand premium on sale. Be balanced.
