## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [firm] Oberoi took a 99-year lease on railway land in Bandra East. What's different about leasehold for a developer?
Answer pointer: no ownership, so buyers get sub-leases. Lenders value leasehold with the tenure in mind. Upfront lease premium vs annual rent. Use restrictions under the lease. Fits commercial/annuity better than for-sale residential (speculation).

### Q2 [appr] List the approvals for a Mumbai high-rise, in order.
Answer pointer: see 03-technical/02. Plan approval (IOD) → CC (plinth, then further CC) → fire NOC stages → environment clearance if above threshold → height clearance → OC. Plus premium payments at each stage.

### Q3 [appr] What's the role of fungible FSI in DCPR 2034?
Answer pointer: up to 35% compensatory area for residential on premium (verify current). It's used for balconies and larger flats.

### Q4 [appr] How would you track 40 approvals across 3 projects?
Answer pointer: an approval tracker (authority, document, status, next action, owner, date, premium due), weekly review, and flags for anything past its SLA.

### Q5 [land] Oberoi prefers owning land outright. Why?
Answer pointer: control, full margin capture, and flexibility in phasing. The cost is capital intensity.

### Q6 [feas] Gurugram: 14.81 acres for ₹597 cr. What's that per acre, and what would you check?
Answer pointer: about ₹40.3 cr/acre. Check permissible FAR (Haryana rules), licence (DTCP), EDC/IDC charges, and sale rate per sq ft in Sector 58.

### Q7 [appr] What happens if an OC is delayed but flats are ready?
Answer pointer: possession can't legally be given. Buyers can claim RERA interest. Reputational damage.

### Q8 [behav] Liaison involves waiting at government offices. How do you stay effective?
Answer pointer: preparation (complete files), relationships with officers built on correctness, and escalation paths. Never promise shortcuts.

### Q9 [feas] Should Oberoi enter Pune with a JDA or outright land?
Answer pointer: a balanced argument. Their history suggests outright, but a new city makes JDAs attractive for local knowledge and lower upfront capital.

### Q10 [appr] What's CRZ and when would it affect a Mumbai project?
Answer pointer: the Coastal Regulation Zone restricts construction near the coastline and creeks. It needs MCZMA clearance.
