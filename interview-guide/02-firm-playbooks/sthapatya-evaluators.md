## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [cap] Three approaches to value. When do you use each?
Answer pointer: sales comparison for flats/land with comps; income for leased assets; cost/DRC for special-use assets and for building value in land-plus-building. See 03-technical/07.

### Q2 [cap] Fair market value vs realisable vs distress value?
Answer pointer: realisable is often 85–90% of FMV, distress 70–80% (common bank practice: assumption, varies by lender policy).

### Q3 [appr] How do you use the ready-reckoner (ASR) rate in a valuation?
Answer pointer: it's the government floor for stamp duty and a reference, not market value. Report both and explain the gap. Maharashtra kept ASR unchanged for FY 2026-27. ([newkerala](https://www.newkerala.com/news/a/maha-govt-maintains-status-quo-ready-reckoner-rates-682.htm))

### Q4 [cf] A lender asks you to certify 45% completion on a tower. How do you verify?
Answer pointer: the BOQ-weighted stage (structure is about 35–40% of cost: assumption), physical check floor by floor, RA bills vs work done, and photos with dates.

### Q5 [land] Valuing agricultural land near an expanding city: what adjustments?
Answer pointer: zoning and NA potential, access, size (large-parcel discount), title clarity, and time to conversion.

### Q6 [cap] What's depreciated replacement cost?
Answer pointer: current cost to build new, less physical, functional and economic obsolescence.

### Q7 [behav] Why valuation after contracting?
Answer pointer: it's the bridge from site knowledge to money, and a route to lending/underwriting roles.

### Q8 [cap] What does an IBBI registered valuer do that an engineer can't?
Answer pointer: statutory valuations under the Companies Act/IBC require a registered valuer in the asset class. Engineers support the work.

### Q9 [land] Carpet vs built-up vs super built-up: what does RERA say?
Answer pointer: RERA defines carpet (net usable area excluding external walls, services shafts, balcony/verandah, open terrace). Sales must be quoted on carpet.

### Q10 [cf] Cost-to-complete for a stalled project: how would you estimate it?
Answer pointer: remaining BOQ quantities × current rates, plus escalation, contingency and rectification of deterioration.
