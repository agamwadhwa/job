## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [feas] A landowner in Nerul has a 1.5-acre plot. What product would you recommend?
Answer pointer: research-first. Catchment incomes, comps' configuration absorption, ticket sizes, and airport-driven demand (speculation). Recommend a mix with a reason for each segment.

### Q2 [feas] How would you estimate the absorption rate for a new project?
Answer pointer: comps' RERA QPR sales per month, adjusted for price point and brand.

### Q3 [firm] How does Xanadu earn money on a mandate?
Answer pointer: typically a fee on sales (a % of revenue), sometimes with a marketing budget pass-through or a performance kicker (typical for this model, unverified).

### Q4 [feas] Pricing strategy for a launch: launch low and escalate, or launch at market?
Answer pointer: launch discount drives early velocity and collections, which fund construction. Then escalate by phase or tower. Trade early IRR against margin.

### Q5 [feas] What data would you put in a one-page micro-market note?
Answer pointer: price trend, launches vs sales, unsold inventory and months of inventory, top 5 projects, and upcoming infrastructure.

### Q6 [jda] Compare a sales-DM mandate with a JDA for the landowner.
Answer pointer: in a sales-DM the landowner keeps development and construction risk and pays a fee. In a JDA the developer takes the risk.

### Q7 [behav] Why research/BD at a sales platform?
Answer pointer: market-facing data on what actually sells, which feeds into feasibility.

### Q8 [feas] What's months of inventory?
Answer pointer: unsold units ÷ monthly sales.

### Q9 [feas] How would you measure a campaign's efficiency?
Answer pointer: cost per lead, lead-to-site-visit, visit-to-booking, and cost per booking.

### Q10 [feas] Mumbai, Pune or Bengaluru: which market would you expand in next?
Answer pointer: pick one and back it with data from IPC reports.
