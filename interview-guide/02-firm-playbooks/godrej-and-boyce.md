## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [firm] G&B pays GPL a 10% DM fee on revenue. Is that fair to the landowner?
Answer pointer: compare the cost of in-house sales/marketing/brand (~5–7% of revenue for marketing and brokerage alone: assumption) plus the brand premium GPL delivers. See case 04-cases/10.

### Q2 [am] Monetise 20 acres at Vikhroli: sell, lease, JDA or develop via DM?
Answer pointer: a DM structure keeps land ownership and most margin. Leasing gives annuity income. Selling crystallises value but loses control. Decide on G&B's capital needs, tax and the mangrove/CRZ constraint.

### Q3 [am] What's NOI and cap rate? Value a 5 lakh sq ft office at ₹120/sq ft/month rent.
Answer pointer: gross rent = 5 lakh × 120 × 12 = ₹72 cr. NOI after about 10% leakage ≈ ₹64.8 cr. At an 8% cap: ≈ ₹810 cr. See 03-technical/06.

### Q4 [appr] Mangroves and CRZ: how do they affect development?
Answer pointer: mangroves are protected, with buffer zones. Development is prohibited or restricted and needs High Court permission in some cases (verify current). They reduce net developable land.

### Q5 [am] Why hold commercial rather than sell residential?
Answer pointer: annuity, REIT optionality, and value creation from a long-term hold.

### Q6 [feas] How would you phase 1,000 developable acres over 20 years?
Answer pointer: infrastructure-first (roads, utilities), a mix of commercial anchors early to lift residential values, and release residential in step with absorption. Avoid flooding the micro-market.

### Q7 [cap] What would a REIT listing need?
Answer pointer: a stabilised, leased commercial portfolio, a sponsor, an independent valuer, and SEBI REIT regulations. Minimum size applies (verify current).

### Q8 [behav] You're from contracting. Why corporate real estate?
Answer pointer: land monetisation brings together construction, approvals and finance, which is where you want to grow.

### Q9 [appr] What does DCPR 2034 say about industrial land conversion in Mumbai?
Answer pointer: conversion rules exist with amenity/housing obligations (verify current regulation and share).

### Q10 [am] Key metrics for managing a leased office portfolio?
Answer pointer: occupancy, WALE, rent reversion, NOI margin, tenant concentration, and capex per sq ft.
