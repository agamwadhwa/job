### Q5 [land] Leasehold vs freehold society plots in Pune: why does it matter?
Answer pointer: leasehold (for example on older government or trust leases) needs lessor NOC and may carry transfer fees. Check the lease deed.

### Q6 [jda] What's in a Pune redevelopment development agreement?
Answer pointer: members' new carpet, amenities, timeline, rent, corpus, bank guarantee, penalty for delay, allotment method, and the developer's rights to sell the free-sale area.

### Q9 [jda] What if the society's PMC is biased towards another developer?
Answer pointer: compete on transparency. Submit a complete tender. Present directly to the GB when allowed.

### Q10 [appr] What approvals come after the development agreement in PMC?
