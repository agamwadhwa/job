## What the IC analyst does
Typical for this role type (unverified for this firm): prepares IC papers for new land/JDA deals, stress-tests BD's feasibilities, tracks post-approval performance against the IC case, and builds portfolio-level capital allocation views.

## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [firm] Wakad JDA at 22.5% revenue share. Compare that with buying the land outright.
Answer pointer: the landowner's 22.5% of GDV is effectively the land cost, paid over time from collections. Compare PV(22.5% of collections) vs the outright price. The JDA preserves capital and shares price risk. See 03-technical/03, problem 1.

### Q2 [feas] 1.9 msf, ₹2,300 cr GDV: what's the implied rate, and is it plausible for Pune?
Answer pointer: ₹2,300 cr ÷ 1.9 msf ≈ ₹12,100/sq ft. Whether that's carpet or saleable isn't stated. For a Lodha-branded project in west/central Pune it's plausible (speculation). Ask which area basis.

### Q3 [feas] What would you challenge first in a BD feasibility at IC?
Answer pointer: price vs comps (brand premium justified?), absorption, approval timeline, and TDR availability and cost.

### Q4 [cap] How should a developer allocate capital across outright land vs JDAs?
Answer pointer: target ROCE and a pipeline mix. JDAs lift ROCE. Outright land in core markets captures more margin.

### Q5 [feas] GDV guidance vs launch guidance vs pre-sales: how do they relate?
Answer pointer: BD adds GDV. Launches convert a share each year. Pre-sales follow launches with an absorption lag.

### Q6 [appr] The liaison role: what's the difference between PMC and PMRDA approvals for a Pune project?
Answer pointer: the planning authority by jurisdiction. Both apply UDCPR. Process times differ (verify current SLAs).

### Q7 [feas] How do you set a post-launch KPI to show whether the IC case is on track?
Answer pointer: bookings vs plan (units, price), collections, cost incurred vs budget, and approval milestones.

### Q8 [cap] What's the right net debt/equity for a residential developer?
Answer pointer: listed developers target low leverage (for example < 0.5×: assumption). Residential cash flows are cyclical.

### Q9 [behav] The role asks for 4–6 years. Why should we consider you?
Answer pointer: be candid. You'd take an analyst-grade role and your model is your evidence.

### Q10 [feas] Pune vs Bengaluru for incremental capital: which and why?
Answer pointer: speculative. Frame it with data you can cite: absorption, price growth and land availability from IPC reports.
