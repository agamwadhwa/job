## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [feas] Highest-and-best-use: a 5-acre plot in Hinjewadi Phase 2. Residential, IT office or data centre?
Answer pointer: zoning and permissible use first. Then demand (IT absorption vs residential), power availability (for a data centre), and residual land value for each option. Pick the highest RLV that's legally and physically possible.

### Q2 [am] Warehouse pre-lease on the Chakan corridor: what's the yield on cost?
Answer pointer: see case 04-cases/06. Rent (e.g. ₹24–28/sq ft/month: assumption) × 12 ÷ total cost per sq ft (land + construction ~₹1,800–2,400/sq ft: assumption).

### Q3 [firm] 11 acres for ₹5,400 cr in MMR in Q1 2026: what does ₹490 cr/acre tell you?
Answer pointer: a very high value per acre implies high FSI and premium pricing, i.e. central Mumbai. Land is roughly 30–40% of GDV in such deals (speculation).

### Q4 [feas] How do you size demand for Grade A warehousing in Pune?
Answer pointer: past absorption (msf/year), occupier pipeline (manufacturing, e-commerce, 3PL), vacancy, and supply under construction.

### Q5 [am] What's a lock-in vs lease term in Indian leasing?
Answer pointer: lock-in = the period neither party can exit (tenant pays rent regardless). Term = total lease with renewals. Escalation typically 4–5% annually or 15% every 3 years (assumption).

### Q6 [feas] Market assessment deliverable: what are the sections?
Answer pointer: macro, micro-market, supply, demand, pricing, competitive benchmarking, SWOT, recommendation.

### Q7 [cap] Why did India's land transactions grow so fast in 2025?
Answer pointer: developer balance sheets, residential demand, and new asset classes (data centres, warehousing). Cite the JLL numbers.

### Q8 [behav] Consulting or transactions: which do you want and why?
Answer pointer: be clear. Consulting for the analytical depth that feeds feasibility work.

### Q9 [am] What's a built-to-suit warehouse?
Answer pointer: a developer builds to a tenant's spec with a long lease commitment. Lower vacancy risk, specific design.

### Q10 [feas] How would you forecast rent growth for Chakan?
Answer pointer: vacancy trend, supply pipeline, and tenant demand. Keep rent growth modest if the supply pipeline is large.
