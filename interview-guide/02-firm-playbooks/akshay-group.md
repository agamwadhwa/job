## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [jda] How would you find redevelopment leads in Andheri West?
Answer pointer: society lists from the cooperative registrar, buildings from the 1970s–90s, brokers, architects and PMCs who run tenders, and residents' groups.

### Q2 [feas] Quick check: a 1,000 sq m society plot. What sale area can we get?
Answer pointer: zonal FSI + premium + TDR per road width under DCPR 2034 (verify current), + fungible 35%, less rehab area. Show structure, give ranges.

### Q3 [appr] What's an IOD and CC in Mumbai?
Answer pointer: IOD (Intimation of Disapproval) = plan approval with conditions. CC = commencement certificate, allowing construction to start. OC at the end.

### Q4 [behav] Tell me about managing a subcontractor on site.
Answer pointer: STAR from your contracting months (06-behavioural).

### Q5 [feas] Our cost is ₹4,500/sq ft of construction area. Is that high for Mumbai?
Answer pointer: for a G+20 residential tower in the suburbs, ₹4,000–5,500/sq ft of construction area is a plausible range (assumption). Depends on basements, podium, spec.

### Q6 [land] What's a property card vs a 7/12?
Answer pointer: property card for city survey (CTS) land in urban areas. 7/12 for agricultural/revenue land.

### Q7 [jda] What does a society worry about most?
Answer pointer: rent being paid on time, finishing the project, and the developer going bust. Solutions: a bank guarantee, an escrow and phase-wise milestones.

### Q8 [behav] You'll handle operations too: approvals chasing, site visits, vendor follow-ups. OK with that?
Answer pointer: yes, and say why it helps: you learn the full cycle.

### Q9 [feas] How do you decide a sale rate for a redevelopment in Andheri?
Answer pointer: RERA comps within 1 km, adjusted for possession date and floor.

### Q10 [appr] What's the role of a liaison consultant?
Answer pointer: files and follows approvals with the MCGM/SRA/collector, and tracks premiums and NOCs (fire, environment, tree authority).
