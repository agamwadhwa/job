## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [feas] Which Mumbai suburb would you pick for a mid-premium redevelopment, and why?
Answer pointer: pick one (for example Goregaon or Malad West). Use infrastructure (metro lines: verify status), price trajectory, redevelopment stock (1970s–80s societies), and competition.

### Q2 [feas] How do you decide unit mix for a 150-unit redevelopment sale component?
Answer pointer: comps' absorption by configuration, ticket-size affordability for the catchment, and rehab constraints on the floor plate.

### Q3 [jda] 33(7)(B) incentive FSI: what is it?
Answer pointer: for societies older than 30 years, incentive BUA of 15% of existing BUA or 10 sq m per tenement, whichever is more, without premium. ([summary of DCPR 33(7)(B)](https://kutirgroup.in/blog/2025/09/24/regulation-337b/)) **Verify current.**

### Q4 [feas] How would you benchmark our pricing against competitors?
Answer pointer: RERA-registered projects within 1–2 km: carpet rate, PLC, floor rise, possession date, sales % from RERA QPRs.

### Q5 [cap] Should a mid-size developer take a fund partner or grow with internal accruals?
Answer pointer: it's a trade-off between speed of growth and control and cost. Tie it to pipeline size.

### Q6 [behav] The posting asks for premier institutes. What's your case?
Answer pointer: don't argue with it. Show specific work: the feasibility model, contracting cost exposure, and your analytics background.

### Q7 [feas] How would you present a Go/No-Go to the promoter in one page?
Answer pointer: see case 04-cases/12. The deal, three numbers (margin, IRR, peak funding), three risks and the recommendation.

### Q8 [appr] Timeline for a Mumbai society redevelopment from agreement to OC?
Answer pointer: typically 1–2 years to approvals + IOD/CC, then 3–4 years of construction (assumption). Say ranges, not a point.

### Q9 [feas] What's the biggest risk in suburban redevelopment right now?
Answer pointer: (speculation) oversupply of redevelopment sale inventory at similar ticket sizes, plus approval delays.

### Q10 [behav] What would you do in your first 30 days?
Answer pointer: learn the live projects' numbers, build a competitive database, and review the last 5 offers made or received.
