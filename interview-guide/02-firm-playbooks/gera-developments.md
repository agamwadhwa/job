## 10 likely questions
Only Q10 comes from a public Gera interview report. The rest are typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [firm] Gera builds in Kharadi, Hinjewadi and Wagholi. Which of the three would you add land in next, and why?
Answer pointer: pick one and defend it with demand (IT employment catchment), supply (unsold inventory, months of inventory), infrastructure (Pune Ring Road, metro extension: verify status) and land cost per sellable sq ft. Wagholi: cheaper land, thinner margin (REF-W shows 16%). Kharadi: established, pricier, and Gera already has a large project there.

### Q2 [feas] 2 acres on an 18 m road in Wagholi. Walk me through sellable area.
Answer pointer: REF-W. Net 7,700 sq m × 2.5 = 19,250 sq m. + 60% ancillary = 30,800 sq m = 3.32 lakh sq ft FSI area. × 0.78 = 2.59 lakh sq ft carpet. State that the road width drives the TDR cap (verify current).

### Q3 [jda] A landowner wants 40% area share. How do you decide?
Answer pointer: compare the landowner's share value with outright land cost. 40% of 2.59 lakh sq ft ≈ 1.04 lakh sq ft ≈ ₹78 cr of revenue at ₹7,500, against ₹16 cr outright. Almost certainly a no; counter at 25–30% or move to a revenue share. See case 04-cases/01.

### Q4 [land] What documents do you check first on a Pune land parcel?
Answer pointer: 7/12 extract and mutation entries (Ferfar, Form 6), 30-year title chain, NA order/zone certificate, DP remarks, and a search report at the Sub-Registrar. Also: encumbrances, Tenancy Act/Inam/Watan class restrictions, ceiling and court cases.

### Q5 [feas] Our IRR hurdle is 20%. Your feasibility shows 23%. What would you stress first?
Answer pointer: sales velocity and price. In REF-W, stretching absorption from 12 to 20 quarters takes IRR from 23.6% to 13.4%. Price −10% takes it to 12.2%. Construction delay matters less.

### Q6 [appr] What's the difference between PMC, PCMC and PMRDA for a project?
Answer pointer: the planning authority depends on location. PMC for city limits (including the merged villages, where UDCPR applies), PCMC for Pimpri-Chinchwad, PMRDA for the metropolitan region outside the corporations (Wagholi was merged into PMC in 2021: verify current).

### Q7 [behav] Your experience is contracting. Why would a land team hire you?
Answer pointer: construction cost realism (you know what ₹/sq ft actually buys), stage-of-work judgement, and working with site teams. See 06-behavioural.

### Q8 [feas] How do you estimate absorption for a new launch in Kharadi?
Answer pointer: bottom-up comps. Units sold per month in 5–8 comparable RERA projects (MahaRERA quarterly progress reports / IPC data), adjusted for brand, product and ticket size. Cross-check with months of inventory at micro-market level.

### Q9 [land] A broker brings you a parcel with a 2 m approach road. Interested?
Answer pointer: not at face value. Access width caps FSI and TDR, and may block high-rise fire approval. Check the DP road proposal and whether a right of way can be bought. Price it as a lower-FSI parcel.

### Q10 [behav] How long are you planning to stick around with us?
Reported as the hardest question in a Gera interview (design role). ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Interview/Gera-Developments-Interview-Questions-E938771.htm)) Answer pointer: be concrete. "Long enough to take a parcel from first screening to launch, which is 2–3 years at minimum. That's the learning I'm here for."
