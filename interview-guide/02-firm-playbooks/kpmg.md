## What this role does
MPA consultant (typical): a PMC-like advisory role covering project controls, schedule and cost assurance, risk registers, contract management, and PMO for large infrastructure or real-estate programmes. RE Advisory covers feasibility, market studies and transaction support.

### Q2 [cf] What's earned value management? Define CPI and SPI.
Answer pointer: EV = budgeted cost of work performed. CPI = EV/AC. SPI = EV/PV. Below 1 means over cost or behind schedule.

### Q3 [cf] A metro project is 8 months behind. How would you diagnose it?
Answer pointer: critical path analysis, delay attribution (client, contractor, force majeure), resource histograms, and a recovery schedule.

### Q4 [feas] RE advisory: how would you structure a feasibility for a government land monetisation?
Answer pointer: HBU, market study, development options, financial model per option, transaction structure (lease, JV, sale), and risk.

### Q5 [cf] What's a risk register and what goes in it?
Answer pointer: risk, cause, impact, probability, owner, mitigation, and residual rating.

### Q6 [cf] What's the difference between a PMC and an owner's engineer?
Answer pointer: a PMC manages delivery for the owner. An owner's engineer gives technical oversight/assurance, often for lenders or owners.

### Q7 [behav] Why consulting and not a developer?
Answer pointer: be honest. Breadth and structured problem-solving, then a move to the developer side (don't hide the plan, but don't lead with it).

### Q8 [cf] Primavera vs MS Project?
Answer pointer: Primavera for large multi-project EPC schedules. MSP for smaller projects.

### Q9 [feas] Guesstimate: how many new flats does Pune need per year?
Answer pointer: a structure: population × growth + household formation + replacement demand, then compare with IPC sales (about 50–90k units/year for Pune: assumption; check the latest IPC report).
