## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [jda] Dahisar: 0.30 acres, 128 units. Roughly how much of that is rehab vs sale?
Answer pointer: you don't know the existing members. Explain how you'd compute it: existing carpet × (1 + offered %) = rehab carpet. Total carpet from FSI minus rehab = sale.

### Q2 [jda] Explain 33(7)(B) incentive to a society secretary in plain words.
Answer pointer: old societies (30+ years) get extra buildable area free of premium: 15% of existing built-up or 10 sq m per flat, whichever is more (verify current). That's what funds your bigger flat.

### Q3 [jda] Our offer is 35% more carpet. A rival offers 45%. How do you win without matching?
Answer pointer: delivery track record, bank guarantee size, rent escalation, shorter timeline, better amenities, and a credible funding plan. The members' real fear is a stalled project.

### Q4 [feas] Rent in Dahisar for 1 BHK is about ₹25,000/month (assumption). 60 members × 42 months?
Answer pointer: 60 × 25,000 × 42 = ₹6.3 cr, plus 5–10% annual escalation.

### Q5 [appr] What NOCs does a suburban redevelopment need before IOD?
Answer pointer: society consent/GB resolution, a tree NOC if applicable, fire, and in some locations CRZ/airport height. Verify the list with the liaison consultant.

### Q6 [land] Society not conveyed: what do you do?
Answer pointer: apply for deemed conveyance under MOFA before or alongside the development agreement. Otherwise the title is weak.

### Q7 [jda] What is a PAAA?
Answer pointer: the permanent alternate accommodation agreement between the developer and each member: new flat details, timeline and rent. Registered.

### Q8 [behav] You'll meet 60 members, all with different demands. How do you manage it?
Answer pointer: one written FAQ, one committee channel, no side promises, and everything minuted.

### Q9 [feas] Hardship allowance, corpus, rent, shifting: rank them by cost impact.
Answer pointer: rent (long duration) > corpus > shifting > hardship. Size them with numbers.

### Q10 [jda] How do you protect the developer if the project stalls because of member litigation?
Answer pointer: GB resolution thresholds, individual consents, a clause that the society handles dissenters, and extension of timelines for force majeure/litigation.
