## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [cf] A developer requests a ₹20 cr tranche on 60% completion. What do you check?
Answer pointer: physical stage vs claim, RA bills and the engineer's certificate, cost incurred vs budget, deviations from sanctioned plan, and RERA QPR consistency.

### Q2 [cap] Explain how you'd value a 2 BHK in a Pune under-construction project.
Answer pointer: comps (resale and new sale in the same project/micro-market), adjusted for stage; check against the ASR rate; report FMV and realisable value.

### Q3 [appr] What documents confirm a building is legal?
Answer pointer: approved plan, CC, OC (for completed buildings), RERA registration for new projects, and a completion certificate.

### Q4 [cf] What's a deviation and why does a lender care?
Answer pointer: construction beyond the sanctioned plan (extra floors, enclosed balconies). Risk of demolition or penalty, and the security is impaired.

### Q5 [cap] FMV vs distress value for LAP?
Answer pointer: distress is typically 20–30% below FMV (assumption). It's used for collateral cover.

### Q6 [cf] Cost-to-complete: the developer claims ₹40 cr remaining. How do you test it?
Answer pointer: bottom-up from BOQ quantities remaining × current rates, plus escalation and contingency. Compare with your own site judgement.

### Q7 [land] Title vs technical: who checks what?
Answer pointer: legal checks title (search, chain, encumbrances). Technical checks physical and regulatory (plan, construction, area, marketability).

### Q8 [behav] You've handled RA bills on site. How does that help here?
Answer pointer: you know how contractors pad measurement and how to verify it. Use a real STAR example (06-behavioural).

### Q9 [cf] Why does an NBFC lend to developers at all when banks are cheaper?
Answer pointer: speed, flexibility on structure, and banks' sector exposure limits.

### Q10 [cap] What are the three biggest technical risks in developer lending?
Answer pointer: approval lapses, cost overruns/stalling, and quality defects affecting saleability.
