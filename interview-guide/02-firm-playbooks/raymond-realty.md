## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [firm] Walk me through our Parel JDA. Is 40% revenue share to the landowner good for us?
Answer pointer: the developer keeps about 60% of revenue (≈ ₹5,100 cr on ₹8,500 cr GDV) and carries construction, marketing and finance. Peak investment is only about ₹700 cr because the landowner bears rehab and approvals and collections fund construction. That's asset-light, so ROCE is high even if the margin % on GDV is modest. Risks: a long 8-year tail, approval timing, and South Mumbai luxury absorption.

### Q2 [jda] Revenue share vs area share: why would Raymond prefer revenue share in Mumbai?
Answer pointer: it keeps pricing and inventory control (one seller, no landowner undercutting on price), makes escrow and accounting cleaner, and the landowner benefits from price upside.

### Q3 [feas] What GDV would you estimate for a 2-acre parcel in Wadala?
Answer pointer: FSI under DCPR (zonal + premium + TDR + fungible: verify current) × sellable efficiency × carpet rate (assume ₹30,000–38,000/sq ft carpet for Wadala premium: assumption). Show the method.

### Q4 [cap] Why does the market value "GDV pipeline" and what's wrong with it as a metric?
Answer pointer: it's easy to add and ignores timing, margin and the developer's share under a JDA. Better: PV of the developer's share of cash flows.

### Q5 [feas] Thane land is owned, with no land cost. How should we price it internally?
Answer pointer: at opportunity cost (market value), not zero. Otherwise margins are overstated and capital is misallocated.

### Q6 [appr] What's a JDA's stamp duty/GST treatment in Maharashtra?
Answer pointer: the JDA/development agreement attracts stamp duty (verify current rate). GST on development rights was restructured in 2019 (verify current). Say "I'd confirm with the tax team" rather than bluffing.

### Q7 [jda] What clauses matter most in a JDA from the developer's side?
Answer pointer: title warranties, the approval CP and timeline, the landowner's cost obligations, the revenue/area split mechanics, escrow, pricing control, termination, step-in and dispute resolution.

### Q8 [behav] Why Raymond Realty over a larger developer?
Answer pointer: a newly listed, fast-growing JDA platform means more deals per head. Tie to the Parel deal.

### Q9 [feas] A society wants ₹X corpus plus 45% more carpet. How do you decide?
Answer pointer: see 04-cases/03.

### Q10 [cap] Raymond Realty's stock is priced on the pipeline. How would one bad JDA affect it?
Answer pointer: (speculation) delays or cost overruns on a flagship deal can erode credibility of the whole GDV figure. That's why diligence quality matters.
