## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [land] You're aggregating 40 acres from 15 farmers near Panvel. What's your process?
Answer pointer: map survey numbers, pull 7/12s and mutation entries, identify the weakest title first, sign MoUs with token payments and exclusivity, run a public notice, and close in a sequence that avoids holdouts at the centre of the parcel.

### Q2 [feas] ₹167 cr for 5 acres in Madurai with 55 plots. What does that imply per plot?
Answer pointer: ≈ ₹3.0 cr of land cost per plot before development cost. Implies high-value commercial/residential plots near the temple. The price per acre (₹33.4 cr) is high for a tier-2 city.

### Q3 [land] What's the holdout problem and how do you manage it?
Answer pointer: the last landowner demands a premium. Manage it by sequencing, confidentiality, option agreements, and a layout design that can work without the holdout.

### Q4 [appr] What approvals does a plotted layout need in Maharashtra?
Answer pointer: NA (non-agricultural use) permission, layout sanction by the planning authority (for example PMRDA/CIDCO/NAINA depending on location: verify current), internal roads and amenity space, and RERA registration for plotted projects.

### Q5 [feas] Why are plotted developments popular with developers?
Answer pointer: low capex (roads, utilities, no buildings), fast cash turnover, and lower construction risk. Downside: lower absolute margin per acre, and buyer financing is harder.

### Q6 [land] What is NAINA and why does it matter near Navi Mumbai?
Answer pointer: the Navi Mumbai Airport Influence Notified Area, planned by CIDCO, with its own development rules and town planning schemes (verify current).

### Q7 [land] How do you respond if a local claims part of the land is government land?
Answer pointer: check the revenue records' history (the old 7/12 and mutation register), the gairan/government classification, and survey measurements (TILR). Don't argue in the field. Escalate to legal.

### Q8 [behav] Land aggregation is relationship-heavy. How have you built trust with local stakeholders before?
Answer pointer: STAR from contracting, dealing with school management and local authorities. [FILL]

### Q9 [feas] Pricing plots: per sq ft of plot vs per sq ft of FSI?
Answer pointer: buyers think per sq ft of plot. Developers should compare with value per FSI sq ft to check against apartment feasibility.

### Q10 [appr] RERA for plotted development: what's registered?
Answer pointer: layouts with more than 8 plots or 500 sq m (verify current threshold) need RERA registration, including a development timeline for infrastructure.
