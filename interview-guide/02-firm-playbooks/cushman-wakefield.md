## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [firm] Our land report shows acreage up about 7.6× from 2021 to 2025. What's driving it, and is it sustainable?
Answer pointer: listed developers' balance sheets repaired after 2021, a residential upcycle, data centres/warehousing needing land, and tier-2 expansion. Sustainability depends on residential absorption holding (speculation).

### Q2 [cap] A corporate owns 10 acres of surplus industrial land in Pune. Outright sale, JDA or JV? How do you advise?
Answer pointer: depends on their cash need, risk appetite and tax. Outright gives certainty now. JDA gives more value later with execution risk. Also check the change-of-use feasibility under UDCPR (industrial to residential: verify current rules and premium).

### Q3 [cap] How do you run a land sale process?
Answer pointer: IM → buyer list → NDAs → data room → site visits → non-binding bids → shortlist → binding bids → SPA/negotiation → close.

### Q4 [cap] Residual land value in one minute.
Answer pointer: GDV − (construction + approvals + marketing + finance + developer profit) = what a developer can pay for land, discounted for time.

### Q5 [cf] Explain LTC and LTV in construction finance.
Answer pointer: LTC = loan ÷ total project cost; LTV = loan ÷ value of security. See 03-technical/05.

### Q6 [cap] What makes an IM credible to a developer buyer?
Answer pointer: a correct area statement, clean title summary, DP remarks and zoning, comps, and a realistic timeline.

### Q7 [cap] How do IPCs get paid on land deals?
Answer pointer: a success fee as a % of transaction value (often 1–2%: assumption), sometimes with a retainer.

### Q8 [feas] What's the key difference in a land deal in a tier-2 city?
Answer pointer: thinner buyer pool, slower absorption, title records less digitised, and plotted development more common.

### Q9 [behav] Why would we hire someone with 14 months of experience into this team?
Answer pointer: into an analyst slot, not this one. Show the model and site cost knowledge.

### Q10 [cap] Structured debt vs equity for a developer's land purchase?
Answer pointer: banks can't fund land. NBFC/AIF structured debt at 16–20% or equity partners. See 03-technical/07.
