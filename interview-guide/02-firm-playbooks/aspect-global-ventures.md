## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [jda] A 30-year-old society in Andheri West with 40 members, each at 500 sq ft carpet. What do you offer?
Answer pointer: see case 04-cases/03. Offer = additional carpet % (often 30–50% in the western suburbs: assumption), corpus, rent, shifting and a hardship bond. It must leave the developer ≥ 18–20% margin on the free-sale component.

### Q2 [jda] 33(7)(B) vs 33(9) vs 33(10): when does each apply?
Answer pointer: 33(7)(B) is existing societies, non-cessed, older than 30 years, with incentive BUA. 33(9) is cluster redevelopment (large-scale urban renewal). 33(10) is slum rehabilitation (SRA). See 03-technical/03. **Verify current.**

### Q3 [land] 1,000 acres near Pawna. What diligence is different at that scale?
Answer pointer: aggregation risk (hundreds of 7/12s), tribal/Class II land restrictions, forest/eco-sensitive zone checks, the Tenancy Act, access and water, and the zoning under the PMRDA DP (verify status).

### Q4 [feas] Plotted development vs villas on that land: which works?
Answer pointer: plotted gives faster cash with lower capex, but sells at a lower value per acre and faces a buyer-financing gap (plot loans are harder). See case 04-cases/04.

### Q5 [appr] What does SRA involve that a normal redevelopment doesn't?
Answer pointer: an eligibility survey (Annexure II), 51%/70% slum-dweller consent (verify current), free rehab tenements, and the sale component tied to rehab completion. Timelines run long.

### Q6 [firm] Why would a bullion/diversified group be good at real estate?
Answer pointer: cash availability and speed of decision. Say it neutrally; don't flatter.

### Q7 [feas] What's premium/fungible FSI under DCPR 2034?
Answer pointer: fungible compensatory area of up to 35% for residential, on payment of premium (verify current), in addition to the zonal FSI and TDR.

### Q8 [land] How do you check whether a society's land is conveyed?
Answer pointer: the property card in the society's name, or deemed conveyance order. Without conveyance, the developer inherits a title gap.

### Q9 [behav] You've never done a society negotiation. How would you prepare for a general body meeting?
Answer pointer: members' concerns (rent, timeline, a guarantee), a comparison sheet of competing offers, the escrow/bank guarantee structure, and a single-page FAQ.

### Q10 [feas] Shivam's top line is ₹3,850 cr. What would you want to know to judge the margin?
Answer pointer: rehab vs sale split, land/redevelopment cost, premiums paid, construction cost for a 215 m tower (about 1.5–2× a mid-rise per sq ft: assumption), and the sales velocity at luxury ticket sizes.
