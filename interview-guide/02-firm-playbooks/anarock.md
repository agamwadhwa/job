## 10 likely questions
Glassdoor's ANAROCK aggregate (about 72.5% positive, difficulty 2.69/5, about 6 days) has no capital-markets breakdown. ([Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.co.in/Interview/Anarock-Interview-Questions-E1905306.htm)) All questions below are typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [cap] A Pune developer needs ₹150 cr for a project where land is paid for and approvals are in. Equity, structured debt or construction finance? Walk me through it.
Answer pointer: with land paid and approvals in, construction finance from a bank or NBFC is cheapest (about 10–12%: assumption). If sales are weak or the promoter needs to fund land elsewhere, go to structured debt/mezzanine (about 15–20%). Equity only when there's no cash flow visibility. Decide on cost of capital against control and security.

### Q2 [cap] How do you value a land parcel for a sale mandate?
Answer pointer: residual land value (GDV − costs − developer margin, discounted) cross-checked against land comps per acre and per FSI sq ft. See 03-technical/07.

### Q3 [firm] We just bought 51% of a design firm. How does that help capital markets?
Answer pointer: (speculation) a design partner can test massing and FSI quickly at the IM stage. That makes a land sale or JDA mandate more credible and faster to underwrite for buyers.

### Q4 [feas] What's a reasonable project IRR for a Pune mid-income residential project?
Answer pointer: unlevered about 18–25%, equity IRR higher with CF leverage (assumption). REF-W gives 23.6% unlevered.

### Q5 [cap] What does an investor look at first in a teaser?
Answer pointer: location/micro-market, approvals status, land cost per FSI sq ft, the developer's track record, the ask (amount, instrument, tenor), expected returns, and security.

### Q6 [feas] Pune sold X units last quarter and unsold inventory is Y. What's months of inventory, and what does it tell you?
Answer pointer: unsold ÷ (quarterly sales/3). Below about 12 months is healthy, above 18–20 is stressed (rule of thumb, unverified).

### Q7 [cf] What's the RERA 70% rule and how does it affect a lender?
Answer pointer: 70% of collections go into a designated account and are used for land and construction cost of that project only. Withdrawals are in proportion to completion, certified by an engineer, architect and CA. Lenders ring-fence the escrow for repayment within those limits. **Verify current.**

### Q8 [cap] Explain IRR vs equity multiple to a landowner.
Answer pointer: the multiple is how many times the money comes back. IRR adds how fast. Use a 2-year 1.5× vs a 5-year 2× example.

### Q9 [behav] Why an IPC and not a developer?
Answer pointer: honest version: an IPC shows many deals, a developer shows depth in a few. Say which you want first and why.

### Q10 [cap] Build a quick IRR: invest ₹100 cr, get ₹40 cr in year 2 and ₹120 cr in year 4.
Answer pointer: solve 100 = 40/(1+r)² + 120/(1+r)⁴. Try 15%: 40/1.3225 = 30.2 and 120/1.749 = 68.6, total 98.8, just under 100, so r is a bit below 15%. Exact: **14.6%**. Show the trial-and-error; the method is what's being tested. Multiple = 160/100 = 1.6×.
