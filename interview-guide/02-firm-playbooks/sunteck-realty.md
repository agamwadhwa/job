## 10 likely questions
Questions are typical for this role type (unverified for this firm). Several are built directly from the JD bullets above.

### Q1 [firm] The Andheri East parcel had a decade of litigation. How would you have diligenced it?
Answer pointer: map every party and every suit (civil, revenue, High Court). Get consent terms or decrees. Title insurance if available. Structure payments as escrowed, tied to each suit's withdrawal or disposal. Get a public notice and objections. Price in time and legal cost.

### Q2 [land] Why buy a company (Tanirika) instead of the property?
Answer pointer: possible stamp duty efficiency (share transfer vs conveyance: **verify current Maharashtra treatment**), faster transfer, and existing approvals/tenancies remain in the SPV. Risk: inherited liabilities, so do full company diligence plus an indemnity.

### Q3 [land] Site visit checklist from the JD: what's a DP road and why does frontage matter?
Answer pointer: a DP road is a road proposed in the Development Plan, which can widen access and raise FSI/TDR eligibility but may take land (road-widening deduction, compensated by FSI/TDR). Frontage drives retail potential, views and building design.

### Q4 [feas] Sales velocity: how would you estimate it for a luxury launch in Andheri East?
Answer pointer: comps' units per month from RERA QPRs, adjusted for ticket size. Luxury absorbs slower, so assume a longer sell-out.

### Q5 [jda] JV vs outright vs DM: when would Sunteck prefer each?
Answer pointer: outright when title is clean and price is right, for full margin. JV/JDA when land is expensive and the owner wants upside. DM when the owner wants to keep risk and pay a fee (about 8–12% of revenue: assumption).

### Q6 [feas] Projections with justified assumptions: justify a price escalation assumption.
Answer pointer: historical micro-market CAGR from IPC data, capped below inflation plus 1–2%. Show a zero-escalation case.

### Q7 [cap] How would a society redevelopment in Andheri East compare with outright land on margin?
Answer pointer: redevelopment has no land cost upfront but pays rehab area, rent and corpus. Margin % can be similar or higher, but timeline risk is larger.

### Q8 [appr] Which approvals would you track for a Mumbai tower?
Answer pointer: IOD, CC, fire NOC, environment clearance above threshold (verify current), height clearance (AAI/civil aviation near the airport, which matters in Andheri East), tree authority, and OC.

### Q9 [behav] GD topic: will redevelopment solve Mumbai's housing shortage?
Answer pointer: structure for-and-against. It adds supply at the high end, but affordability comes from SRA and peripheral supply.

### Q10 [cap] The corporate strategy team asks: land bank vs asset-light, which should Sunteck prioritise?
Answer pointer: the trade-off is ROCE vs margin capture. Recent deals mix both.
