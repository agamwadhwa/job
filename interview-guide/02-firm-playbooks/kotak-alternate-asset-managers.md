## 10 likely questions
All typical for this role type (unverified for this firm).

### Q1 [cap] A Pune developer asks for ₹200 cr of structured debt for a mid-income project. What are your top five diligence items?
Answer pointer: (1) title and approvals status; (2) the developer's delivery track record and group leverage; (3) project cash flow: sold vs unsold, receivables, cost to complete; (4) security package (mortgage, escrow, share pledge, personal guarantee); (5) exit: repayment from sales, refinance.

### Q2 [cf] Define cost-to-complete cover and what level you'd want.
Answer pointer: (receivables from sold units + funding available) ÷ remaining cost. ≥ 1.2–1.3× before new lending (assumption).

### Q3 [cap] Why would a developer take 18% money from a fund instead of 11% from a bank?
Answer pointer: banks don't fund land or early stage; speed; flexibility; banks' sector limits.

### Q4 [cap] What protects the fund if sales slow?
Answer pointer: minimum price/velocity covenants, cash sweep, step-in rights, additional security, a DSRA, and cross-collateral from other projects.

### Q5 [feas] Walk me through REF-W and tell me where you'd lend.
Answer pointer: peak funding is ₹54.7 cr around Q5 (TDR loaded in Q3, early construction before collections). Lend against land + approvals + TDR, with repayment from collections from Q7 onwards. Cover comes from project surplus of about ₹36 cr before finance.

### Q6 [cap] Gross IRR vs net IRR for LPs?
Answer pointer: net deducts management fee, carry and expenses. The gap is typically 3–5 percentage points (assumption).

### Q7 [firm] ADIA and NPS Korea are LPs. What do global LPs care about in Indian residential credit?
Answer pointer: governance, currency-adjusted returns, loss history, ESG, and the RERA-ring-fenced cash flows that make residential credit safer.

### Q8 [cf] How does RERA's 70% account affect a lender's repayment?
Answer pointer: repayment can come from the 70% account only for loans taken for that project's land/construction (verify current). Structure the collection flow to hit the escrow first.

### Q9 [behav] You have 14 months in contracting. What do you bring to a fund?
Answer pointer: cost-to-complete and site-progress judgement that most MBA associates lack.

### Q10 [cap] How would you monitor a ₹150 cr investment monthly?
Answer pointer: sales MIS (units, price, collections), escrow statements, construction progress via the lender's engineer, RERA quarterly updates, and a covenant compliance certificate.
